# The deliberate mess in the synthetic data

All three inputs are generated by `reconciler/generate.py` (seed in `config.py`). Nothing is real: the
organization is not named, and every vendor, product, price and person is invented. Each item below is on
purpose, and a test or a report section depends on it.

Products (all invented): Cedar Suite (office suite), Cedar Assist (AI assistant add-on), FieldForm Pro
(survey and data collection) and FlowLoop (automation platform). Centres: Zahle, Jbeil, Saida, Tripoli and
head office. Departments: Social, Medical, Logistics.

| Mess | Where | What a careless pipeline does | What this one does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Staff moved between centres | `JBE-007` moves from Jbeil to Saida on 2026-05-12 (`events/`). The Jbeil office-suite seat goes to a replacement, Saida opens a seat | Counts the person in two centres, or two people on one seat | One seat per seat id; the Jbeil seat is split 11 and 20 days, the Saida seat starts on the move date |
| Seat reassigned mid-month inside one centre | `S-HQ-ASSIST-05` on 2026-04-17 | Counts two people, so two seats | One seat, split by day, one seat price in total |
| Invoice file imported twice | `invoices/invoice_ASSIST_2026-04 (1).csv`, same bytes as the original | Doubles April for the AI add-on | Recorded in `raw_files.duplicate_of`, adds no rows |
| Usage report a month late | `usage/usage_TRI_2026-08.csv`, due 2026-09-05, received 2026-10-06 | Treats Tripoli seats as unused | Ignored before its arrival, so every Tripoli seat-month for August is `unknown` |
| Late report changes the answer | `TRI-002` silent May to July, active in August | Reclaims a seat from someone who came back | Candidate on the early data, not a candidate once August arrives |
| Late onboarding of one product at one centre | Saida assigns 12 AI add-on seats on 2026-03-04; use starts in July. Saida's office suite, survey tool and the other centres are normal | Calls Saida the weakest centre and reclaims seats | Detects the start month per product and centre, holds the seats back |
| Seats billed that nobody holds | 2 AI and 2 office-suite seats at head office, 1 AI at Jbeil, 1 survey at Zahle | Ignores them or fails to reconcile | Shown as paid, nobody assigned |
| A user missing from a report | `ZAH-004` absent from the Zahle July report for the AI add-on | Reads absence as zero use | `unknown` |
| Dirty ids | Jbeil June report: lower case with a trailing space | Users fail to join | Trimmed and upper-cased in staging |
| A repeated row | `HQ-002` twice in the head office July report for the office suite | Double counts the person | First row kept |
| Unordered log | `events/assignment_events_*.csv` is shuffled | Wrong holder at the end | Ordered by date and event id inside the SQL |

Product is part of every key. A seat id belongs to one product, invoices are reconciled per product and
centre, usage rows join on product, and the forecast pool only holds seats of the product under decision.
